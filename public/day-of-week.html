Week numbers and day of the year

Week numbers follow the ISO 8601 standard used across Europe and in most business software: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week that contains the year’s first Thursday. That means the first days of January can belong to the last week of the previous year, and the last days of December can belong to week 1 of the next.

The day of the year counts January 1 as day 1. A leap year, such as 2024 or 2028, has 366 days because it includes February 29. A year is a leap year when it divides by 4, except for century years, which must divide by 400. So 2000 was a leap year and 2100 won’t be.

Questions

What day of the week was I born on?

Enter your date of birth. The calendar leaf and the summary show the weekday.

Why is my week number different from my calendar?

Some calendars, mostly in the US, start weeks on Sunday and count the week of January 1 as week 1. This calculator uses the ISO standard.

How far back does it work?

Any date from year 1 to year 9999, using the modern (Gregorian) calendar throughout.

Privacy policy

What Dayspan collects, how ads use cookies, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 25, 2026.

Calculator data

The dates you enter are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. They appear in the page address so results can be bookmarked and shared, which means they can show up in your browser history.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Local storage

The site stores two small values in your browser: when the full-screen ad was last shown, so it appears at most once a day, and whether you closed the corner video during this visit. They never leave your device.

Server logs

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

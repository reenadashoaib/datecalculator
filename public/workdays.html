How workdays are counted

Every Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday from the start date up to the end date counts as one workday. Saturdays and Sundays are left out. As with the days calculator, the start date is included and the end date is only included if you tick Count the end date too.

Public holidays are not removed, because they differ between countries, states and employers. Subtract the holidays that apply to you from the total.

Questions

How many workdays are in a year?

Most years have 260 or 261 weekdays, and leap years can have 262. Enter January 1 and December 31 with the end date counted to see the exact number for any year.

How many workdays are in a month?

Between 20 and 23, depending on the month and which weekday it starts on. Enter the first and last day of the month and tick “Count the end date too”.

Can I find the date 10 workdays from now?

Yes, use Add or subtract, set Days to 10 and tick “Count days as workdays”.

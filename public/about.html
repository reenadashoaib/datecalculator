About Dayspan

Dayspan is a set of free calculators for counting days and working with dates.

Dayspan answers the everyday date questions that are fiddly to do by hand: how many days until something, how old someone is, which date is 90 days from now, and how many workdays sit between two dates.

Every calculation runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us, and results appear as soon as you change a date.

How we keep results accurate

Dates are counted on the calendar, not by clock time, so daylight saving changes never add or remove a day. Months and years are counted as calendar months and years, and leap years follow the Gregorian rules. Each calculator explains its method under the result.

How the site is funded

Dayspan is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads are labeled “Advertisement”. See the privacy policy for how ad partners use cookies.

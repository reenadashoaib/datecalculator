About

Percentage Calculator is a free set of tools for the percentage sums that come up every day: discounts, tips, tax, grades, pay rises and price changes.

Every calculator works in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere or stored.

How the answers are worked out

Each result shows the formula with your numbers filled in, so you can check it or do the same sum by hand next time. The formulas are explained, with examples, on the main page.

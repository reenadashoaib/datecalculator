How age is calculated

Age is counted in whole calendar years and months from the date of birth, then the remaining days. Someone born on May 20 turns a year older on each May 20, and is “1 month older” on the 20th of each month.

People born on February 29 have a birthday on February 28 in years that aren’t leap years. Change Age on to see someone’s age on a past or future date, for example on the day of an exam or a retirement date.

Questions

How old am I in days?

Enter your date of birth. The “In days” row shows how many days you have been alive, counting today as not yet finished.

When is my next birthday?

The “Next birthday” row gives the date and the weekday it falls on, and how many days are left until then.

Can I work out an age on a past date?

Yes. Change “Age on” to any date after the date of birth.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing you type is sent to a server. The date does appear in the page address so you can share or bookmark the result.

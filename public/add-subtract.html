How the date is worked out

Years and months are added first, then weeks, then days. Adding a month keeps the same day of the month: March 10 plus 1 month is April 10. If that day doesn’t exist in the new month, the result moves to the month’s last day, so January 31 plus 1 month is February 28 (or February 29 in a leap year).

Weeks are always 7 calendar days. Days are calendar days unless you tick Count days as workdays; then Saturdays and Sundays are skipped, and the start date itself isn’t counted. Adding 1 workday to a Friday gives the following Monday.

Common uses

  • A due date 30, 60 or 90 days after an invoice
  • A deadline 10 workdays from today
  • The end date of a 6-month contract or a 12-week program
  • The date 280 days (40 weeks) after a given day

Questions

What is 90 days from today?

Leave the start date on today, set Days to 90 and keep Add selected. The result shows the date, its weekday and its week number.

Why does adding 1 month to January 31 give February 28?

February has no 31st, so the result moves to the last day of February. This is the usual convention for contracts and billing.

Can I combine years, months, weeks and days?

Yes. Fill in as many as you need. They are applied in that order, largest first.

Are public holidays skipped in workday mode?

No. Only Saturdays and Sundays are skipped, since holidays differ by country and region.
